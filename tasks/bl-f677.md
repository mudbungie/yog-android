Measured live 2026-09-28 (yog bl-65dc phone leg): the engine saw each punched stream close ~5 s after HOME; on return the app read 'Software caused connection abort (os error 103)' and re-entered the ladder at rung 4, 30–90 s per return. Android suspends a backgrounded app's sockets unless a foreground service holds them (or the app is exempt from battery optimization). Design decision: (a) a foreground service that keeps the held line and its 25 s pings alive while the app is backgrounded — costs a persistent notification and battery, buys instant return and the attention fetch over the held line; or (b) accept the death, but make the RETURN cheap: rung 3 (re-punch at the cached endpoints) must land in seconds, not a new rendezvous — the cache survived here (same engine endpoints) but the engine's side needs a fresh call to punch back, so rung 3 alone cannot succeed against an engine that only punches on a call. Read yog REMOTE §13.3–§13.4 and state which; if (b), the fix is a lighter re-call path (write the call, punch) that skips the presence read.
