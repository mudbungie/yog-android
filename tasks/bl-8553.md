yog landed the `enroll` act (yog bl-f4e3) and with it two additions to the wire vocabulary: `request/enroll` and `reply/enrolled`, plus new rows in `corpus/shapes.json`. `PROTOCOL` was **not** bumped — REMOTE §3 strict decode already refuses an unknown op in band, and the drift ledger records both at the standing version — so nothing here is broken today.

But the vendored `corpus/` in this repo is now stale, and `tests/conformance/` is exhaustive **in both directions**: a shape with no row and a row with no shape are each a red test. So re-vendoring is not a copy — it is two decisions to write down.

The likely answers, to be confirmed against the corpus rather than assumed:

- **`reply/enrolled`** — this client will want to decode it the day it grows a Lernie-side *mint* screen (a seated operator enrolling the next device from this phone). It does not have one, so today it is a recorded skip that refuses **naming itself**.
- **`request/enroll`** — an operator-grade Action. A foot may never send it (REMOTE §4.2 is a closed set and `src/foot.rs` makes a fourth gesture a compile error), and the seat has no screen that would. Same: a recorded skip that refuses by name.

**What is already conformant and needs no work:** `src/envelope.rs` was built to the contract as stated and REMOTE §8's payload section now records it verbatim — compact JSON, the six fields under `"yog-enroll":1`, grade spelled `operator` / `foot`, PEM verbatim rather than DER-plus-base64. Checked field by field against the landed `corpus/reply/enrolled.json`; they agree.

**One measurement worth carrying over** for the decoder ball (bl-d815): REMOTE measures a real mint at **1567 bytes** of compact JSON (P-256, 825-day leaves), which a version-40 QR carries at error-correction level L, M or Q and **not** at H. So the rule a scanner must meet is *PEM as minted, at level M or lower* — H needs a smaller envelope and there is no payer for one. Whatever decoder is adjudicated has to read a version-40 symbol; that is the bar.
