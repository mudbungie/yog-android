yog bl-d1f1 stated the wire's lost-reply recovery contract in yog REMOTE §3
(new bullet ahead of "The disk inbox survives for in-world callers"): a
connection that dies between the engine completing an act and the reply frame
landing leaves the act IN DOUBT — no idempotency token rides the act
envelope, no redelivery slot exists for acts, and the recovery is a read of
the world, never a resend. Nothing wire-visible moved: PROTOCOL stands at 8,
the corpus is unchanged, nothing to re-vendor.

What the phone seat owes it — behavioral only:

1. Never auto-retry an act after a transport error (a dropped connection, a
   write that failed mid-frame, a read timeout after the act was written).
   Retrying a read is fine (yog REMOTE §9.7: asking twice is asking once);
   retrying an act is a second act (§9.8: two clicks of Nudge are two nudges).
2. Surface the failure as *in doubt* rather than *failed* where the act had
   been written before the transport died: the engine may have completed it,
   and the next read of the conversation/roster shows whether it did. This
   matters more on a phone than on the desktop — mobile radios drop
   connections routinely, so the tempting reconnect-and-replay loop is
   exactly the unsafe path the contract forbids.

Landed as DESIGN §19. Every act path was audited; no auto-resend existed
anywhere and none was added, so the work is a class, three fates, a paint and
two fences.

The resend audit, path by path:
- seat acts (message, prepare+prompt, stop, nudge, model pick, effort,
  priority): one gesture per operator command, fired once by the worker, never
  requeued. Unchanged in that respect.
- the foot's `complete`: the capture is moved into the gesture and dies with
  the channel; the redial presents and reads afresh and carries nothing over.
  Unchanged, now stated and fenced.
- the foot's `advertise`: re-asserted on every fresh channel and after every
  hand-off. Lawful and kept — a presentation is idempotent and the engine's
  `wrote` reports it. Two of the three foot gestures may be repeated; the
  completion is the one that may not.
- the seat's cadence pass, the live tail, the selector reads, the scheduled
  fetch: all asks, all re-asked freely (REMOTE §9.7), and untouched.

Completion loss: no dedupe, matching thrall's DESIGN §3.8 rather than
suppressing. The engine requeues a dropped claim (yog bl-e658), so the work
comes back on the next `invocations`, this device runs the tool again and
answers the new delivery. The alternative — remembering ids — is memory across
exactly the gap the redial exists to cross.

Where the doubt begins: `Wire` gained a fourth class, `Lost`, and the line is
the framing's rather than a guess. A write that errored is bytes not accepted,
and the reader takes a length header then exactly that many bytes without
scanning, so a frame that failed mid-write was never decoded; a frame written
whole cannot be known to have arrived. `transport()` (dial again?) says yes to
both channel classes; `in_doubt()` (may it have run?) only to `Lost`. The
engine's preface stays definite because the listener writes its version before
it reads a request frame.

The defect found, and it was one tap wide: a lost reply counted as a refusal,
so the composer's echo handed the operator's text back into the field — one tap
from a second copy of a message the engine may already hold, on a device whose
radio drops connections routinely. An act in doubt now keeps its echo standing,
marked, with the contract under it and the read named; the transcript the seat
re-reads every cadence dissolves it if it landed. A state and not a control:
no resend button, so no act: tag.

Every act names its own read (transcript / the row's flight / the conversation
list / the assignments read the worker makes anyway), worded once in
seat::posted.

Proven: a hangup on the `complete` leg (the redial writes advertise and never a
second completion, then answers the redelivery); a hangup under each seat act
(the sentence names the act and its read, one gesture on the wire); an engine
refusal painted definite rather than doubtful; a socket that never opened and a
handshake that never verified both not in doubt; the echo's three fates and the
one that must not become a draft. make check green, 100% coverage.
