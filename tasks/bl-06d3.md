Round-1 multitenant lane, driving the phone seat against a live engine.

## What the screen shows

A workspace where one conversation had dispatched two named children. The
phone's conversation list painted three sibling rows in age order:

    SepiaTempo • · now
    Coordinate two subagents that must talk to each other.

    Builder • · now
    You implement. Do NOT start yet: you have no spec. Wait for a message …

    Speccer • · now
    You write specs. Write SPEC.md describing a python CLI greet.py …

Nothing says `Builder` and `Speccer` are children of `SepiaTempo`, and nothing
says how many rows are subagents rather than things the operator started.

## What the engine already sends

The same workspace, read from a desktop seat at the same moment:

    SepiaTempo      depth 0  in-flight
    Builder         depth 1  live
    Speccer         depth 1  in-flight
    SlipperAcorn    depth 0  quiescent
    BriskDriftwood  depth 0  quiescent

`depth` is a field on the conversations row. The list has it and does not use
it.

## Why it matters on a phone specifically

A fan of N candidates or a subagent tree is the one shape where the row count
explodes, and the phone is the seat with the least room for it: on this run a
three-agent tree filled a third of the screen with rows that all look like
top-level work. Adjudication, stopping and messaging all address the tree, and
the operator cannot see the tree.

## Expected

Some rendering of `depth` — an indent, a marker, or a collapse under the
parent. Any of the three is one field read.

## Severity

p3. Nothing is unreachable; the list is just misleading about what it is
listing, and gets more misleading the more the suite's subagent features are
used.
