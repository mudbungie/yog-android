The seat enrollment screen lists three deliveries, first among them "a cable — adb push the files into this app's storage". On the release-signed APK (the one the channel ships and `deploy-phone` installs when the key is present) `adb push` into `/data/user/0/<pkg>/files/wire` answers permission denied: only a debuggable build lets the shell write app-private storage. Observed 2026-10-08 (ops bl-13cb). Either the screen stops promising it (the paste field and the scanner are the two real doors), or the shell grows a door that takes the files over the bridge — an intent with a content URI, say — and the screen names that.
