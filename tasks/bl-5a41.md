REMOTE §8.3's act and its held tail landed at protocol 8 (yog bl-61bf), and
`parity.toml` carries `login` and `login-tail` as unbuilt. They were cited to
bl-f645 while that ball was open; bl-f645 built the admin screen's five ops and
re-cited them here, because this is a different piece of work.

**What it is.** A provider row on the composer's selector already states its own
credential fact and stays tappable (DESIGN §13.2, bl-0267) — *"the operator may
be about to sign it in, and a control that vanishes teaches nothing"*. This ball
is the act behind that: fire `login` for the picked provider in the focused
workspace, and follow what `bz --login` prints.

**Why it is not a fifth admin control.** `login-tail` is a THIRD held lane
(DESIGN §14.1) beside the attention queue and the follow: `login` answers a
`login` reply carrying lines, an `outcome` code and a `fallback` sentence, and
the tail is buffered from the start and then live to the settled exit. That is
lane work — `seat::lane`'s `Subject` grows an arm, `pass::adopt` grows a fold,
and the pass has to decide when the lane should stand — not a button.

**What closing this needs.** The `login` reply decoded (its `lines` carry an
`err` flag per line, and the frame may carry `outcome` and `fallback`); the act
fired off the provider row that states the credential fact; the tail held while
a sign-in is in flight and dropped when it settles; and the two `parity.toml`
lines deleted.
